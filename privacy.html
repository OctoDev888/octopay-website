Waitlist privacy

This covers the information you give us when you join the OctoPay waitlist on this website. The OctoPay app will have its own privacy policy when it launches.

What we collect

  • Your email address.
  • Your first name, if you choose to give it.

We don't ask for, and you should never send us, bank details, card numbers, your Social Security number or your date of birth through this website.

How we use it

Only to email you about OctoPay's launch and early access. We never sell your personal data, and we don't use it for ads.

Who handles it

Your sign-up is stored with the email service we use to run the waitlist, which processes it on our behalf. We don't share it with anyone else.

Unsubscribing and deleting your data

Every email we send has an unsubscribe link. To have your sign-up deleted entirely, email support@getoctopay.com and we'll remove it.

Changes

This is a draft and may change before launch. If it changes in a way that matters, we'll update this page.
